Participation rules and privacy policy

This survey was developed as part of a research project at HEC Montréal.

Please answer all questions in this survey as honestly as possible. Some questions may require more thought than others, so take the time you need to respond carefully.

  • To navigate through the survey, please only use the “Continue” button at the bottom of the screen.
  • Please do not use your browser’s navigation buttons (especially the “Back” button).
  • Once you move forward during the survey, there is no option to go back.

The information collected will be anonymous and will remain strictly confidential. Your data will only be used to advance knowledge and to present overall results in academic or professional forums.

Collected data may be shared with other researchers for non-commercial research purposes in projects other than this one. The online data collection provider agrees not to disclose any personal information or other information about participants to any other users or third parties, unless you explicitly consent or if required by law.

Your participation is completely voluntary. You are free to refuse to participate and may stop answering questions at any time. By completing this questionnaire, you are considered to have given your consent to participate in our research project and for the potential use of data collected from this questionnaire in future research. Because the questionnaire is anonymous, it will not be possible to withdraw your data from the study after you have submitted your responses.
