In this survey, you will make investment decisions and receive a bonus that depends on your wealth at the end of the investment task. It is thus important that you read the following explanations carefully.

Structure of the investment task

You can invest an initial wealth of $10,000 across five different investment options, labelled Option 1 to Option 5, over {{ totalRounds }} periods, from Period {{ firstRound }} to {{ investRounds }}.

The task has two phases. Periods {{ firstRound }} to -1 are observation periods, during which you can only observe how the prices of the options evolve. Periods 0 to {{ investRounds }} are investment periods, during which you can invest. Uninvested wealth does not earn any interest.

Investment options

Each of the five options has a starting price of $100 per unit. In each period, the price of each option can either increase or decrease – it cannot remain the same. The following table shows the increase or decrease of each option's price in each period, as well as the chance of a price increase.

For each option, price changes are independent across periods. In addition, the price changes of Options {{ independentAssets.0 }}, {{ independentAssets.1 }}, {{ independentAssets.2 }}, and {{ independentAssets.3 }} are independent of one another. However, Options {{ correlatedAssets.0 }} and {{ correlatedAssets.1 }} tend to move in opposite directions: the price of Option {{ correlatedAssets.0 }} tends to decrease when the price of Option {{ correlatedAssets.1 }} increases, and vice versa. Formally, these two options have a correlation coefficient of {{ corrCoeff|to1 }}.

Before the survey, a computer randomly determined the price changes for each period according to the probabilities shown in the table. This means that your choices cannot influence how prices change.

Investing in the investment options

In each investment period, you decide how to divide your wealth across the five options. For each option, you can either enter the share of your total wealth (in %) or the number of units you want to hold. Any remaining wealth is not invested.

Your portfolio keeps the wealth share (in %) from one period to the next until you change them: after prices have moved, your portfolio is automatically rebalanced to the same percentages. In Period {{ investRounds }}, all units are sold at the prices of that period.

Investment advice

In Periods 0 and 5, you have the option to receive investment advice from a financial advisor.

We have run a similar survey previously with actual financial advisors. Your financial advisor has seen exactly the same price changes as you will have seen until the respective period. In addition, your financial advisor has given the advice to someone with the category of age, gender, and risk tolerance that you fall into.

End of the investment task

The investment task ends in Period {{ investRounds }}. All units of the options you still hold in Period {{ investRounds }} will be sold automatically at the price of Period {{ investRounds }}. Your bonus payment will be equal to the final wealth that your initial hypothetical $10,000 investment has generated, divided by {{ exchangeRate }}.
