In this survey, you will make investment decisions and receive a bonus that depends on your wealth at the end of the investment task. It is thus important that you read the following explanations carefully.

Structure of the investment task

You can invest an initial wealth of $10,000 across five different investment options, labelled Option 1 to Option 5.

The task has two phases. Periods {{ firstRound }} to -1 are observation periods, during which you can only observe how the prices of the options evolve. Periods 0 to {{ investRounds }} are investment periods, during which you can invest.
Uninvested wealth does not earn any interest.

Investing in the investment options

In each investment period, you decide how to divide your wealth across the five options. For each option, you can either enter the share of your total wealth (in %) or the number of units you want to hold. Any remaining wealth is not invested.

From one period to the next, the number of units of each option that you hold stays the same until you change it. Because prices change, the share of your wealth (in %) invested in each option changes from one period to the next.

Investir dans les options d’investissement

À chaque période d’investissement, vous décidez comment répartir votre richesse entre les cinq options. Pour chaque option, vous pouvez indiquer soit la part de votre richesse totale (en %), soit le nombre d’unités que vous souhaitez détenir. La richesse restante n’est pas investie.

D’une période à l’autre, le nombre d’unités de chaque option que vous détenez reste le même jusqu’à ce que vous le modifiiez. Comme les prix varient, la part de votre richesse (en %) investie dans chaque option change donc d’une période à l’autre.

End of the investment task

The investment task ends in Period {{ investRounds }}. All units of the options you still hold in Period {{ investRounds }} will be sold automatically at the price of Period {{ investRounds }}. Your bonus payment will be equal to the final wealth that your initial hypothetical $10,000 investment has generated, divided by {{ exchangeRate }}.
