This experiment is about making investment decisions. You will receive a bonus that depends on your wealth at the end of the investment task. It is thus important that you read the following explanations carefully.

Structure of the investment task

During the investment task, you have five different investment options (Investment Option 1 to Investment Option 5).

The investment task consists of {{ totalRounds }} periods (Period {{ firstRound }} to {{ investRounds }}). In Period {{ firstRound }} you receive a hypothetical wealth of $10,000. You cannot invest your hypothetical wealth in periods {{ firstRound }} to 0. Instead you observe the price development of the investment options. From Period 0 onwards, you may invest your hypothetical wealth.

Investment options

Each of the five investment options has a starting price of $100 in period {{ firstRound }}. Afterwards, the price of each investment option can either increase or decrease. The following table shows by how much the price of each investment option can increase or decrease, as well as the chance of a price increase:

The price changes of Investment Options 1 to 4 are independent of each other, while the price changes of Investment Option 5 are always the opposite of the price changes of Investment Option 1.

Moreover, all price changes are independent of previous periods. Before the experiment, a computer has randomly determined the price changes for each period according to the specific probabilities of each investment option. This also means that your choices do not influence how prices change.

Investing in the investment options

In periods 1 to {{ investRounds }}, you can adjust your investment choices in each period. You can buy additional units of any investment option as long as the money in your account exceeds the price of one unit of the investment option. Similarly, you can sell units of any investment option that you hold at the current price.

Investment advice

In Periods 1, 5, and 10, you have the option to receive investment advice from a financial advisor.

The financial advisor has seen exactly the same price changes as you will have seen until the respective period. In addition, the financial advisor has given the advice to someone who has similar characteristics as you in terms of age, gender, and income.

{{ elif treatment == 2 }}

Investment advice

In Periods 1, 5, and 10, you have the option to receive investment advice from a financial advisor.

The financial advisor has seen exactly the same price as you will have seen until the respective period. In addition, the financial advisor has given the advice to someone who has similar characteristics as you in terms of age, gender, and income, and the financial advisor had access to a generative AI system, such as ChatGPT, when giving the advice.

{{ elif treatment == 3 }}

Investment advice

In Periods 1, 5, and 10, you have the option to receive investment advice from ChatGPT.

ChatGPT has seen exactly the same price changes as you will have seen until the respective period, and knows the explanations on this page.

{{ endif }}

End of the investment task

The investment task ends after Period {{ investRounds }}. All units of the investment options you still hold in Period {{ investRounds }} will be sold automatically at the price of Period {{ investRounds }}. Your bonus payment will be equal to the final wealth that your initial hypothetical £10,000 investment has generated, divided by 20,000.
