In this survey, you will give investment advice to two hypothetical customers.

While you give the advice for hypothetical customers, we will—in a separate survey—show your advice to participants with the same characteristics as your hypothetical customers, called advisees.

Your bonus depends on the advice you give to the hypothetical customers. It is thus important that you read the following explanations carefully.

The advisees' investment task

The advisees have five different investment options (Investment Option 1 to Investment Option 5). They will see the same price development of the investment options over {{ totalRounds }} periods (Period {{ firstRound }} to {{ investRounds }}) as you.

The advisees cannot invest in periods {{ firstRound }} to 0. Instead the advisees will observe the price development of the investment options. From Period 0 onwards, the advisees may invest in the investment options and change their investment choices in each period. Any uninvested wealth is transferred to a risk-free asset and does not earn any interest.

Investment options

Each of the five investment options has a starting price of $100 in period {{ firstRound }}. Afterwards, the price of each investment option can either increase or decrease. The price changes of all investment options except for Investment Option {{ correlatedAssets.0 }} and Investment Option {{ correlatedAssets.1 }} are independent of each other. Investment Options {{ correlatedAssets.0 }} and {{ correlatedAssets.1 }} are correlated with a correlation coefficient of {{ corrCoeff|to1 }}, which means that the price of Investment Option {{ correlatedAssets.0 }} tends to decrease when the price of Investment Option {{ correlatedAssets.1 }} increases, and vice versa.

Moreover, all price changes are independent of previous periods. Before the experiment, a computer has randomly determined the price changes for each period according to the specific probabilities of each investment option. This also means that the advisees' choices do not influence how prices change.

How to give advice

In Period {{ adviceRounds.0 }} and in Period {{ adviceRounds.1 }}, you can give advice to the advisees. The advice will consist of three parts:

  • Situation: Start the advice by briefly summarizing the situation, mentioning client or asset characteristics as you wish.
  • Allocation: For each investment option (1–5) and the risk-free asset, specify the recommended portfolio weight (%).
  • Reason: Please give a short explanation for the advice.

Your bonus

At the end of the survey, you will learn whether you receive a bonus. The chance of receiving a bonus is 10%. If you receive a bonus, it will be around $50 on average, but it can be higher or lower depending on the advice you give.

Your bonus equals the final wealth of one hypothetical customer, divided by {{ exchangeRate }}. Each hypothetical customer starts with $10,000 in Period {{ firstRound }} and follows your advice in every period, rebalancing to your recommended allocation in each period. After Period {{ investRounds }}, all investments are sold at the final prices.

Please note that it has already been determined at the beginning of the survey whether you receive a bonus or not, and which hypothetical customer will be used to determine the bonus. Your advice, however, can affect the size of your bonus.
